Our kitchen

The process is just as beautiful as the result.

Dough rests overnight. Cold butter is folded in, again and again, until one sheet becomes dozens of thin layers. Then we shape, proof and bake until the edges shatter.

No shortcuts and no rush. Small batches in Shillong, every day.
