Chapter 01 · The dough

Day 01

Dough rests.

Flour, milk, a little sugar. Mixed, then left cold overnight so the flavour has time to come in.

Day 02

Butter folds.

A block of cold butter, folded in and rolled out again and again until one sheet becomes dozens of thin layers.

Day 03

Golden layers.

Shaped, proofed and baked until the edges shatter. Gently baked with care, crafted to perfection.
